# Wellmark PPO Demo Benefits 2026

## Page 1 Urgent care
In-network urgent care visits are covered with a $40 copay after no deductible. Out-of-network urgent care may have 40% coinsurance after deductible.

## Page 2 Primary care
In-network primary care visits are covered with a $25 copay. Preventive care is covered at no cost when in-network.

## Page 3 Prescription drugs
Generic prescription drugs are covered with a $10 copay at preferred retail pharmacies.
